SOL THRiVE • Evidence-Based Methodology

Our Approach

Where behavioral science, complexity sense-making, and human-centered AI unite to extend clinical presence and triple client outcome velocity across the 167 hours between sessions.

At THRiVE Coaching, our approach is anchored in evidence-based models that cultivate growth, resilience, and meaningful transformation. We believe that thriving, both personally and organizationally, begins with understanding the systems that shape our behaviors, decisions, and relationships. By drawing from three complementary frameworks, the Strengths Model, the CYNEFIN Framework, and the Heart and Soul of Change, we provide a holistic and adaptable foundation for coaching.

Each of these models offers unique insights into the human experience. Together, they create a dynamic roadmap for growth that honors individual strengths, navigates uncertainty, and places human connection at the heart of sustainable changes.
