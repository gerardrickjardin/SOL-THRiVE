The Core Transformation Curriculum

Each module layers grounded clinical behavioral science (SOL Theory, RellaFit, CYNEFIN, and The Strengths Model) with applied Personos AI execution across the 167 hours between sessions.

Week 01

Sensing the Landscape: CYNEFIN, The 167-Hour Bottleneck & The Observer Stance

Session 01 • Monday, Dec 7, 2026 • 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM MT (90 min)
▾

Diagnose why 1 hour a week stalls across the other 167 hours. Apply SOL Theory, RellaFit, and the CYNEFIN Framework to shift clients from the reactive character caught in the "Ugg" of friction to the grounded Observer.

Core Learning Objectives:
  • Distinguish between Clear, Complicated, Complex, and Chaotic life friction across the 5 clinical domains.
  • Shift clients from the reactive character to the grounded Observer using SOL Theory and RellaFit.
  • Run safe-to-try micro-experiments using the "Probe-Sense-Respond" method.
Diagnostic & Tools:
  • Complete Gerard’s Baseline Flow Survey Triage (Agency, Clarity, Momentum).
  • Workstation speech-to-text microphone calibration (Session 0 Pre-Flight).
  • Baseline Friction & Dossier export for Squad A, B, or C.
Week 02

Activating Your Core: The Strengths Model, THRiVE Report & Safe-Save Protocol

Session 02 • Monday, Dec 14, 2026 • 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM MT (90 min)
▾

Shift permanently from deficit-fixing to strengths amplification. Guide clients through the 20-to-40-minute voice-driven THRiVE Report across all 6 pillars—enforcing the strict 3-to-5 item quota and pressing Enter every 1–2 minutes for the Safe-Save mechanic.

Core Learning Objectives:
  • Uncover Pillar 1 (Traits: innate superpowers) and Pillar 2 (Hobbies: flow-state energy generators).
  • Map Pillar 3 (Relationships: support cast & accountability mirrors) and Pillar 4 (Interests: curiosity compass).
  • Synthesize Pillar 5 (Values: non-negotiable operating system) and Pillar 6 (Experiences: crucible moments & highlight reels).
Diagnostic & Tools:
  • Voice-driven THRiVE Report Strengths Interview in Personos AI.
  • Mandatory Safe-Save Protocol (Enter every 1–2 minutes) into client memory profiles.
  • Strict 3-to-5 item quota verification across T-H-R-I-V-E.
Week 03

Session 03 • Monday, Dec 21, 2026 • 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM MT (90 min)
▾

Move directly from self-awareness into Goal Achievement and Problem Solving (Filling the G.A.P.S.). Configure Personos AI to generate 5 creative next steps tailored through custom client lenses (philosophy, scripture, or thought leaders).

Core Learning Objectives:
  • Master the Master AI Coaching Prompt and custom client lens architecture.
  • Apply G.A.P.S. problem solving across addiction recovery, housing, workforce, emotional distress, and conflict.
  • Guide clients to narrow 5 AI-generated options into 1–3 committed micro-actions.
Diagnostic & Tools:
  • Personos AI Custom Lens Generator (Philosophy, Scripture, Thought Leaders).
  • Audio mindset narration via ElevenLabs TTS integration.
  • 167-hour between-session accountability check-in routines.
