PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT, RELEASE OF INFORMATION & WAIVER OF LIABILITY

This Participation Agreement, Release of Information, and Waiver of Liability (“Agreement”) is entered into between SOLab Studios (“Company,” “we,” or “us”) and the undersigned participant (“Participant” or “you”) governing your enrollment and participation in The THRiVE Method™️, including the 21-Day Sprint, the Personos (“Pur”) digital assistant, coaching sessions, and THRiVE Squad peer communities (collectively, the “Program”).

1. Therapeutic Growth Experience — NOT Therapy or Medical Care

You understand and acknowledge that while The THRiVE Method™️ is grounded in behavioral science and strength-based frameworks—and is designed to be therapeutic, restorative, and growth-oriented—the Program is NOT psychotherapy, mental health counseling, psychiatric treatment, substance abuse treatment, or medical care.

  • No Clinical Relationship: SOLab Studios is an educational and personal development organization, not a licensed healthcare facility. Even if a founder, facilitator, or Coach holds a clinical degree or professional license (including Dr. Steve Huff, PhD), they are acting strictly in an educational, coaching, and peer-facilitation capacity—not as your therapist, counselor, or healthcare provider.
  • No Diagnosis or HIPAA Relationship: Nothing in the Program, your THRiVE Report, your Flow Survey, your Flow Score, or your ActionBoard constitutes a clinical diagnosis, medical advice, or treatment plan. Participation does not create a therapist-patient relationship or a HIPAA-covered clinical record.

2. Artificial Intelligence (“Personos / Pur”) Limitations & Crisis Protocol

The Program incorporates Personos (“Pur”), an AI-powered digital assistant designed to support personal development alongside human connection. By using Personos, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • Automated Software Tool: Pur is an artificial intelligence software tool—not a human being, licensed therapist, or medical professional.
  • Independent Judgment & Technical Availability: AI-generated reflections, summaries, and ActionBoard suggestions may contain inaccuracies or omissions. You remain solely responsible for exercising your own judgment before acting on any output. SOLab Studios does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of third-party AI or learning platforms.
  • No Real-Time Monitoring & Emergency Protocol (988 / 911): Interactions with Pur are not monitored in real time by a clinician or SOLab Studios staff. The THRiVE Method™️, Personos, THRiVE Coaches, and THRiVE Squads do NOT provide crisis intervention or emergency services. If you experience thoughts of self-harm, suicide, harm to others, or a medical or psychiatric emergency, you agree to immediately stop using the Program and call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), call 911, or go to the nearest emergency room.

3. Authorization for Release of Information & Squad Confidentiality

A foundational principle of The THRiVE Method™️ is “Nobody Plays Alone.” To facilitate collaborative coaching and peer support:

  • Authorization to Share Program Materials: You hereby authorize SOLab Studios, its certified THRiVE Coaches, and Personos to access, display, and discuss your Program materials—including your THRiVE Report, Flow Survey responses, Flow Score, and ActionBoard—with your assigned THRiVE Coach and your assigned THRiVE Squad peers for educational and peer-support purposes during the Program.
  • Participant Controls & Privacy: Outside of your assigned Coach and Squad, SOLab Studios will not sell or publicly disclose your personally identifiable responses without your consent, except as required by law or to protect safety. You are advised not to input sensitive third-party medical, financial, or confidential information into Personos.
  • Mutual Squad Confidentiality: You agree to hold all personal stories, challenges, and identifying information shared by other Squad members in strict confidence and never record, screenshot, or disclose them outside your Squad without explicit consent.

4. Peer-to-Peer Squads & Assumption of Risk

Following initial onboarding with a certified THRiVE Coach, you will transition into peer-to-peer collaboration with fellow participants in a THRiVE Squad.

  • Peers Are Not Professionals: You understand that Squad teammates are fellow participants working on their own personal development—not licensed clinicians, legal advisors, or financial professionals. SOLab Studios is not responsible or liable for statements, opinions, or suggestions made by fellow participants.
  • Voluntary Assumption of Risk: Personal development requires active effort and real-world action, and specific personal, career, or relationship results cannot be guaranteed. You voluntarily assume full responsibility for your physical, mental, and emotional well-being and for all decisions and actions you take while executing your ActionBoard.

5. Program Fees, Conditional 50% Risk-Split Guarantee & Sponsored Seats

  • 21-Day Sprint Fee ($1,000): Unless your seat is funded by an approved third-party sponsor or scholarship, the Program fee is $1,000, covering onboarding, access to Personos (“Pur”), your THRiVE Report and Flow Survey, certified THRiVE Coach onboarding, and initial Squad placement for the 21-Day Sprint.
  • Ongoing THRiVE Squad Membership ($20/month): Upon completing the 21-Day Sprint, you may continue belonging to your lifelong THRiVE Squad and using Personos for $20 per month. You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time prior to your next billing date with no penalty.
  • Conditional 50% Risk-Split Guarantee: SOLab Studios splits the financial risk of the 21-Day Sprint only for Participants who paid their Sprint fee directly out of pocket and satisfy all of the following completion requirements:
    • Complete the 6-Pillar THRiVE Report and Day-1 baseline Flow Survey;
    • Set up and actively track your ActionBoard throughout the 21-Day Sprint;
    • Attend and participate in your scheduled onboarding coaching and Squad check-ins; and
    • Complete the Day-21 Flow Survey demonstrating no measurable increase in your overall Flow Score compared to your Day-1 baseline, and submit a written refund request within 7 calendar days following Day 21.
    If all four conditions are verified, SOLab Studios will refund 50% ($500) of your direct out-of-pocket Sprint payment. Participants who fail to attend, fail to complete the required onboarding and Day-21 assessments, or withdraw without completing the Sprint are not eligible for a refund.
  • GoFundMe, Scholarships & Third-Party Funded Seats: If your enrollment was funded in whole or in part through a GoFundMe campaign, crowdfunded pool, scholarship, employer sponsorship, or third-party grant, you are not eligible for any cash refund or payout under any circumstances. Any unused or forfeited sponsored funds remain with the SOLab Studios scholarship fund.
  • No Improper Chargebacks: Access to Personos, digital materials, and cohort onboarding constitutes immediate delivery of services. You agree to resolve any billing questions directly with SOLab Studios in writing and not to initiate unwarranted credit card chargebacks or payment reversals.
